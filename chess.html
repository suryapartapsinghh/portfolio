How it works

Every rule

Full legal move generation with castling, en passant and promotion. Draws by stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule and insufficient material.

Search

Negamax with alpha-beta pruning, capture-first move ordering and a short quiescence search, deepening one level at a time until a one-second budget runs out.

Evaluation

Material plus piece-square tables: a score for what each side has and where it stands. Simple on purpose, which is why it has no long-term plans.

No libraries

Plain JavaScript in one file. The search runs in a Web Worker, so the page never freezes while the bot thinks.
