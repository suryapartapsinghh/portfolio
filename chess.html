How it works. Plain JavaScript, no libraries. Full legal move generation including castling, en passant and promotion; draw detection for stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule and insufficient material. The search is negamax with alpha-beta pruning, capture-first move ordering and a short quiescence search, deepening until a one-second budget runs out. It runs in a Web Worker so the page never freezes.

Evaluation is material plus piece-square tables, which is why it develops sensibly in the opening and goes a little aimless in quiet endgames.
