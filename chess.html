How it works
Rules
Full legal move generation including castling, en passant, and promotion. Draws by stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule, and insufficient material.
Search
Negamax with alpha-beta pruning, capture-first move ordering, and a short quiescence search, deepening iteratively until a one-second budget runs out.
Evaluation
Material plus piece-square tables, which is why it develops sensibly in the opening and goes a little aimless in quiet endgames.
Runtime
Plain JavaScript, no libraries. The search runs in a Web Worker so the page never freezes while it thinks.
